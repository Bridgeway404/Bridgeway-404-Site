Privacy Policy

Bridgeway 404 · Metro Atlanta, Georgia · Effective: November 2026

1. Who we are

Bridgeway 404 ("Bridgeway 404," "we," "our," or "us") is a locally owned junk removal, property cleanout and furniture service business operating in Metro Atlanta, Georgia, and surrounding areas. Our services include junk removal, furniture and mattress removal, garage, basement, attic, apartment and property cleanouts, curbside pickup, furniture assembly, and cleanout services for property professionals. We also run Service Saturdays, a community program with Sistamatic Organizing.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information submitted through our website at bridgeway404.com (the "Site").

2. Information we collect

When you submit a form or contact us through the Site, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, phone number, email address, and your preferred contact method.
  • Service location: your address or general area.
  • Service details: a description of the items or job, approximate volume, item location, access details, and timing.
  • Commercial inquiries: company name, your role, business type, property locations, approximate number of properties, anticipated volume, timeline, and any vendor requirements you share.
  • Service Saturdays nominations: information about you (the nominator) and about the senior you nominate — their first name, general area, whether they know about the nomination, the kind of help needed, and a short description of the situation. We ask nominators not to include medical or financial details.
  • Community inquiries: organization name, contact details, and how you'd like to help.
  • Media: photos or videos you choose to send us by text message or email to support your request.
  • Consent records: a record that you agreed to this Privacy Policy when you submitted a form.

We do not collect payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, government identification, or financial account information through this Site.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request or inquiry and follow up by phone, text, or email.
  • To schedule and coordinate services.
  • To evaluate commercial partnership inquiries and set up vendor relationships.
  • To review Service Saturdays nominations, contact nominators and nominees, and coordinate the program.
  • To track leads and customer interactions in our internal records.
  • To send confirmations, reminders, or follow-up messages related to your request.
  • To improve the quality and responsiveness of our services.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you.

4. We never sell your data

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded, and it is never shared with third parties for their own marketing or advertising. We do not sell leads. We share information only as described in Section 5.

5. How we share information

We use the following service providers, each handling information only on our behalf:

  • Netlify, Inc. hosts the Site and receives and stores form submissions. See netlify.com/privacy.
  • Google LLC (Google Workspace / Google Sheets) may store lead information in private spreadsheets accessible only to authorized Bridgeway 404 team members. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver the font files. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Zapier, Inc. or Make may transfer form submissions from Netlify to our internal records.

Service Saturdays: nomination details are used only to run the Service Saturdays program and never for marketing. We may share nomination and community-inquiry details with our program partner, Sistamatic Organizing, solely to evaluate nominations and coordinate the program. If a nominated senior has not yet been told about a nomination, we speak with the nominator before contacting the senior.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Bridgeway 404 or others.

6. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the requested service or evaluate your inquiry, and for reasonable follow-up periods afterward. If you ask us to delete your information, we will remove it from our active records within a reasonable time, subject to any legal or operational retention requirements.

7. Data security

The Site uses HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit. Our internal records are protected by access controls limited to authorized team members. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable precautions.

8. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics tools that collect personal data. If we add such tools, we will update this policy and provide notice on the Site.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your personal information from our records.
  • Opt out of further communications.

If you were nominated for Service Saturdays by someone else, you may ask us to delete the nomination at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12. We respond to valid requests within 30 days.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
