Questions

Good to know

How much will my job cost?

Every job is different, so we price each one individually based on what you have, how much space it takes up, where it's located and how easy it is to reach. Send a few photos and a short description and we'll confirm your price with you before any work begins.

What's the fastest way to get a quote?

Fill out the quote form (about a minute on your phone), then email photos to info@bridgeway404.com. Photos let us price accurately without a site visit in most cases.

Is there anything you can't take?

For safety and compliance reasons we don't currently accept refrigerators or other Freon appliances, paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, hazardous materials, biohazards, asbestos, unknown leaking containers, or anything that requires a separate permit to handle. See the full list.

How soon can you come out?

Tell us your timeline when you request a quote — same day, next day, this week or flexible — and we'll confirm the earliest window we can offer.

Do you work with property managers and other businesses?

Yes. We're actively building ongoing relationships with property managers, investors, realtors, attorneys, storage facilities and contractors. Learn about commercial partnerships.

Do you offer moving services?

Our focus is junk removal, cleanouts and furniture assembly. We can also provide labor-only help — for example, moving heavy items within your home or loading a truck you've arranged. Moving services that transport household goods are not offered through this website.

Ready for a cleaner tomorrow?

Tell us what needs to go. We'll get back to you with a clear price and a time that works.
