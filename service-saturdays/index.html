A community initiative from Bridgeway 404 and Sistamatic Organizing — helping older neighbors across Metro Atlanta clear out, settle in and feel at home again, when program capacity allows.

What it is

Neighbors helping neighbors

On select Saturdays, our two teams volunteer their time to help qualifying seniors clear out what they no longer need and organize what they keep — at no cost to the senior.

Over the years, a garage, basement or spare room can fill up until it's hard to use — and hard to tackle alone. Service Saturdays brings the muscle and the know-how so seniors can enjoy more of their home again.

Why we do it

Serving a stronger Atlanta

"A Cleaner Tomorrow" isn't just our slogan. The 404 is home, and we believe a growing local business should give back to the community that supports it — starting with the neighbors who've been here the longest.

Bridgeway 404

Brings the crew, the truck and the heavy lifting — carrying out and hauling away the furniture and items the senior has decided to let go.

Sistamatic Organizing

Brings professional organizing expertise — helping sort, decide what stays, and set up the space so it's easier to live in and keep tidy.

Who it's for & how it works

How nominations work

Service Saturdays is intended for Metro Atlanta seniors who need a hand and meet the program's guidelines. Because each Saturday has limited capacity, every nomination is reviewed individually.

  1. Nominate

    A family member, friend, caregiver, community organization — or the senior themselves — submits the short form below.

  2. We review

    Our teams review each nomination against the program guidelines and the capacity we have available.

  3. We reach out

    If it looks like a fit, we contact you to learn more, confirm eligibility and make sure the senior is on board.

  4. Service Saturday

    If the senior is selected and a date is available, we schedule a visit and get to work together.

Please noteA nomination is not an appointment and doesn't guarantee free service. Eligibility, scheduling and the scope of each visit depend on program guidelines and available capacity. Upcoming dates are shared directly with nominees and partners as they're confirmed.

Questions

Does Service Saturdays cost anything?

No. Help provided through Service Saturdays is free for seniors selected for the program.

When are the next Service Saturdays?

Dates are scheduled based on team and partner availability. We share upcoming dates directly with nominees and community partners as they're confirmed, and announce them on Instagram at @bridgeway404.

Can I nominate myself?

Yes. Choose "I'm nominating myself" on the form.

Need help sooner, or don't qualify?
