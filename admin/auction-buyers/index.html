Auction Buyer Leads

People and companies that recently bought property at a tax sale, levy sale, sheriff’s sale or foreclosure in Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, Henry or Douglas. The default view is the call queue: who to call next, why, and which number to use. Direct means the number belongs to the buyer, their company or a verified principal. Indirect means it reaches a law firm, agent or related company that may connect us to the buyer — the row says exactly who answers. Leads with no usable number stay under Research More. Tap a number to dial, set the status, jot a note, pick a follow-up date. Not Interested and Bad Lead are hidden unless you choose to show them. Research runs automatically every Wednesday morning.

The pitch (why we are calling)
“Congratulations on the property at [address]. We are Bridgeway 404, a local cleanout crew — we clear out everything left behind after a tax sale or foreclosure so you can get straight to renovating, renting or listing it. Can we give you a quick quote?”

Repeat purchasers are the best leads: an investor who buys at every sale needs a cleanout crew on call.
