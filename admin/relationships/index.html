Import research CSV

Bulk-apply research to people. Columns: person_key or ig_username (to match), plus any of: company, title, industry, location, relevance_tier (1–3), opportunity_score (0–100), research_confidence (confirmed/high/medium/low/unknown), why_matters, recommended_action, action_status, action_priority, next_action_date, notes. Blank cells never erase existing data.

Imported datasets

Deleting an import removes its relationship signals and any people who have no other signals and no manual research. People you have researched are kept.

Privacy

This tool holds personal contact data from Michael's own accounts. It is only readable by allowlisted Bridgeway admins (row-level security), is never indexed or public, and phone numbers are shown only inside a person's detail view. Never commit exports to the code repository. To remove someone entirely, open their profile and use Delete person.
