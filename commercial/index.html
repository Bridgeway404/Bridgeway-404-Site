The right help for your properties

Different businesses need different things from a cleanout vendor. Here's how we fit in.

Property managers & apartment communities

Units can't be cleaned, repaired or re-leased until left-behind belongings are gone — and bulk items in breezeways and dumpster areas don't wait.

How we help

  • Turnover and move-out cleanouts scheduled around your make-ready calendar
  • Bulk-item pickups from breezeways, curbs and dumpster areas
  • Tell us your access process — leasing office, lockbox or on-site staff — and we'll work around it
  • One point of contact across all of your properties
Start a property-management inquiry

Eviction attorneys & landlord clients

Once a landlord client has lawful possession, the unit needs to be cleared promptly and handled professionally.

How we help

  • A cleanout vendor you can refer landlord clients to with confidence
  • We take direction from the landlord or property manager on what is removed
  • A calm, discreet crew for sensitive move-outs
  • Coordination with your client on timing after possession

Bridgeway 404 does not provide legal services or carry out evictions; removal happens only at the property owner's direction.

Start an attorney referral inquiry

Storage facility operators

Delinquent and abandoned units sit unrentable until someone empties them — and leftovers after a sale still have to go.

How we help

  • Unit cleanouts once your lien process is complete and you authorize removal
  • Leftover contents cleared after sales and auctions
  • Several units handled in a single visit when your schedule allows
  • Units left empty and ready for your team to inspect
Start a storage-facility inquiry

Real estate investors & realtors

Acquisitions and listings often come with someone else's belongings, and every day of holding costs money.

How we help

  • Whole-property cleanouts before rehab, photos or showings
  • Garages, sheds, attics and basements included
  • A walk-through with you or your agent to confirm what stays
  • Quotes from photos so you can plan without waiting on a site visit

Straight answers

Questions vendors usually get

Have a unit to clear right now? Call 404-565-4216 or start an inquiry.

What we can't removeRefrigerators and Freon appliances, paint, chemicals, fuel, propane, hazardous materials, biohazards, asbestos, leaking containers, or anything that needs a special permit. If a unit contains any of these, tell us up front. Full list
Do we have to sign a long-term contract?

No. You can start with a single job to see how we work. If recurring work makes sense, we'll talk through an arrangement that fits your schedule.

How is pricing handled?

Each job is quoted individually from photos or a description and confirmed with you before any work begins. For recurring work across several properties, we can discuss a standing arrangement.

Can you work when no one from our team is on site?

Tell us your access process — lockbox, leasing office, gate codes or on-site staff — and we'll confirm how we can work with it.

Can you meet our vendor onboarding requirements?

List them in the inquiry form (for example a W-9, certificate of insurance or vendor-portal registration) and we'll respond with exactly what we can provide.

What areas do you cover?

Metro Atlanta and surrounding communities. Put your property locations in the form and we'll confirm coverage.
