Pricing & quotes

How much does junk removal cost in Atlanta?

The cost of junk removal depends on how much you have, what the items are, and how easy they are to reach. Bridgeway 404 prices every job individually and confirms the price with you before any work begins, with no obligation. A single couch or mattress costs much less than a full garage or whole-home cleanout.

The fastest way to get your exact price is to request a free quote and send a few photos.

How do you calculate the price of a junk removal job?

We look at four things: what the items are, how much space they'll take up in our truck, where they are (curbside, in the garage, upstairs, in a basement or attic), and how easy they are to reach. Stairs, elevators, parking and tight hallways all affect the work, so mention them when you request a quote and we can price it accurately the first time.

Are junk removal quotes free?

Yes. Requesting a quote is free and there's no obligation. We confirm your price, and a time window that works for you, before any work begins.

Can I get a junk removal quote from photos?

Yes, and it's the fastest way. Fill out the quote form (about a minute on your phone), then text photos to 404-565-4216 or email them to info@bridgeway404.com. In most cases, photos let us price the job accurately without a site visit.

Will the price change on the day of the job?

Your price is based on what you describe and show us. If there's noticeably more or less on the day, we'll tell you before we load anything and confirm the updated price with you first. No surprises.

Scheduling & service area

Do you offer same-day junk removal in Atlanta?

Yes, when our schedule allows. When you request a quote, choose same day, next day, this week or flexible, and we'll confirm the earliest window we can offer. For the fastest answer on a same-day job, call 404-565-4216.

What areas do you serve?

Bridgeway 404 serves Atlanta and the surrounding Metro Atlanta communities, including Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Smyrna, Roswell, Alpharetta, East Point, College Park, Stockbridge and Lithonia. Availability depends on distance, timing and job size. If your area isn't listed, just ask.

Do I need to be home for junk removal?

Not always. For curbside or driveway pickups, you may not need to be there. Mention it in your quote request and we'll confirm. For work inside a home, someone needs to let us in and confirm what's going. That can be you or someone you trust, such as a family member, property manager or realtor.

How long does junk removal take?

It depends on the size of the job. A single-item or curbside pickup is quick, while a full garage, basement or whole-home cleanout takes longer depending on volume and access. We give you a time window when we confirm your price, and a heads-up if anything changes on the day.

Do I have to move items to the curb first?

No. We remove items from wherever they sit, including bedrooms, upstairs rooms, basements, attics, garages and storage units. You point, we lift. If you've already put items at the curb or in the driveway, that works too.

How should I prepare for my junk removal appointment?

Decide what's going and set aside anything you want to keep. Let us know about stairs, elevators, gate codes or parking ahead of time. On the day, clear a walking path if you can and keep pets in a separate room. We handle the lifting and loading.

What we take

What items do you take?

Most household junk and bulky items, including couches and sectionals, recliners, mattresses and box springs, dressers, desks, tables, bed frames, boxes and bagged items, clutter, old exercise equipment, décor, and the contents of garages, basements, attics and storage units. Not sure about something? Send a photo and ask before you book.

What items can't you take?

For safety and compliance reasons, we don't currently accept refrigerators or other Freon appliances, paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, hazardous materials, biohazards, asbestos, unknown or leaking containers, or anything that requires a separate permit to handle. See the full list.

Do you remove couches and mattresses?

Yes. We remove couches, sectionals, recliners, mattresses and box springs from wherever they sit, whether it's a single piece or a whole room's worth. Get a furniture removal quote in about a minute.

Do you pick up just one item?

Yes. We do single-item pickups and curbside or driveway piles at a scheduled time, so you don't need a full load to book us.

Do you take refrigerators or other appliances?

We don't currently take refrigerators or other appliances that contain Freon. For other appliances, send a photo with your quote request and we'll confirm whether we can take it.

Do you haul away construction or renovation debris?

We can take light, non-hazardous debris and leftover materials. Tell us what it is and roughly how much, and we'll confirm whether it's within our capabilities. We can't take anything containing asbestos, paint, chemicals or other hazardous materials.

Will the City of Atlanta pick up my bulk trash or old couch?

City of Atlanta residents can request bulk-item collection through ATL311 (dial 311 or 404-546-0311). Pickups must be scheduled in advance, and the city limits what it takes and how often, so check with ATL311 for the current rules.

Bridgeway 404 can help when you can't get the items to the curb, live outside city limits, have more than the city will take, or need it gone on a specific day.

Cleanouts

What is included in a whole-house or estate cleanout?

We clear a home from top to bottom, including garages, sheds, attics and basements. Before we start, we walk through with you or your agent to confirm what stays. We work at a respectful pace and check with you about anything you might want to keep.

Can you clean out a loved one's home or an inherited property?

Yes. We help families, executors and their agents with estate and inherited-property cleanouts, and we know these jobs can be emotional. We work carefully and respectfully, check with you about anything you might want to keep, and leave the home ready for the next step, whether that's a sale, a rental or a renovation.

Do you do garage, basement and attic cleanouts?

Yes. Garages, basements, attics and storage rooms fill up over the years. We clear out what you no longer want so you can use the space again. Learn more about cleanouts.

Can you help with an apartment move-out cleanout?

Yes. If you're moving out, we remove leftover furniture and items so you can hand over the keys on time, including in buildings with stairs or elevators. Mention the floor, elevator access and parking in your quote request.

Do you clean out storage units?

Yes. We can empty your own storage unit, and we also work with storage facility operators to clear delinquent or abandoned units once the lien process is complete and the facility authorizes removal. Storage operators can start here.

Furniture assembly

Do you assemble IKEA, Wayfair and Amazon furniture?

Yes. We assemble flat-pack furniture, including pieces from retailers like IKEA, Wayfair and Amazon: bed frames, dressers, desks, shelving, TV stands, tables and patio sets. We put it together cleanly and correctly, in apartments, houses and offices across Metro Atlanta.

How much does furniture assembly cost?

Assembly is priced by the type and number of items and how complex they are. Drawers, glass, mirrors and wall-mounting all add work. Send the item type, quantity, and a product link or photos through our assembly request form, and we'll confirm a clear price before we start.

Moving & labor help

Do you offer moving services?

Our focus is junk removal, cleanouts and furniture assembly. We can also provide labor-only help, for example moving heavy items within your home or loading a truck you've arranged. Moving services that transport household goods are not offered through this website.

Can I hire you just for heavy lifting?

Yes. For labor-only help, we can move heavy items from room to room, load or unload a truck or container you've arranged, or rearrange furniture. Tell us what you need and we'll let you know honestly whether it's a fit.

Property professionals

Do you work with property managers, realtors and investors?

Yes. We work with property managers, apartment communities, real estate investors, realtors, eviction attorneys, storage facilities, contractors and commercial property owners. You get one local crew and one point of contact across your properties. There's no long-term contract: start with a single job and see how we work. Learn about commercial partnerships.

Do you do rental turnover and eviction cleanouts?

Yes. We clear left-behind furniture and belongings so units can be cleaned, repaired and re-leased, and we schedule around your make-ready calendar. For evictions, we clear the unit after the landlord has lawful possession, taking direction only from the property owner or manager. Bridgeway 404 does not provide legal services or carry out evictions.

Can you meet our vendor requirements, like a W-9 or certificate of insurance?

List your requirements, such as a W-9, certificate of insurance or vendor-portal registration, in the partner inquiry form. We'll respond with exactly what we can provide.
